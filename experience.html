Selected projects

My contributions

01

Longitudinal prediction

Delivery-Time Forecasting from Maternal and Fetal Monitoring Data

My contribution

  • Developed and iterated temporal models combining fetal heart rate, uterine contraction signals, gestational age, clinical variables, and patient history.
  • Implemented CNN–Transformer signal representations and patient-level LSTM components for repeated, irregularly timed measurements.
  • Explored population priors, empirical-Bayes ideas, probabilistic outputs, prediction intervals, and short-horizon delivery risk.
  • Designed patient-level evaluation and information-safe retrospective testing to reduce leakage from repeated measurements or future information.
  • Analyzed overall, near-delivery, term, and preterm performance; maintained training and inference code, including four-GPU distributed training.
02

Heterogeneity & rare outcomes

Preterm Risk and Population-Aware Model Iteration

My contribution

  • Compared specialist models, soft mixture-of-experts designs, and a unified full-data model for term and preterm populations.
  • Investigated clinical and learned-representation features for model routing and different expert-training strategies.
  • Evaluated measurement-level preterm probability and seven-day delivery risk while separating patient-level from measurement-level conclusions.
  • Identified small-sample and class-imbalance limitations and corrected interpretations that exceeded the available holdout evidence.
03

Physiological signals

Fetal Heart Rate Signal Processing and Algorithm Evaluation

My contribution

  • Reviewed an inherited fetal phonocardiography pipeline from preprocessing and template matching through RR intervals and heart-rate outputs.
  • Investigated signal dropouts, output rate, smoothing, quality gating, rescue search, and template-based recovery strategies.
  • Detected inconsistent 250 Hz and 500 Hz sampling assumptions and implemented recognition and adaptation logic.
  • Improved timestamp alignment, missing-value handling, off-body detection, and state-control logic while preserving physiological time continuity.
  • Kept accuracy claims tied to the availability of reliable reference signals rather than treating continuity alone as evidence of correctness.
04

Measurement science

Neonatal Bilirubin Device Validation and Calibration Analysis

My contribution

  • Analyzed paired measurements across multiple devices and batches, focusing on systematic differences, error distributions, and measurement-level effects.
  • Used Bland–Altman reasoning and device-, batch-, and range-level analysis to distinguish average bias from individual agreement.
  • Reviewed calibration designs for split independence, repeated-device leakage, parameter identifiability, reference-device dependence, and stability.
  • Translated statistical findings into bilingual technical reports, management summaries, and recommendations for further validation.
05

Research infrastructure

Biomedical Data Auditing and Human Review Tools

My contribution

  • Queried medical databases and traced relationships among measurement IDs, participant records, signal URLs, and local files.
  • Built resumable download, deduplication, integrity-checking, signal-pairing, and automated quality-screening workflows.
  • Audited cross-channel sampling rates, sample counts, durations, common coverage, missingness, and flat signals.
  • Created a local review dashboard for signal visualization and structured human quality assessment.
