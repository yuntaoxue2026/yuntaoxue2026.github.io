Longitudinal prediction

Delivery-Time Forecasting

Physiological signals

Fetal Heart Rate Signal Processing

Highlight · Why it matters

Identified and addressed inconsistent sampling assumptions and failure states that could otherwise distort heart-rate timing and continuity.

Key contributions

  • Audited the full pipeline from preprocessing and template matching to RR intervals and heart-rate output.
  • Detected conflicting 250 Hz and 500 Hz assumptions and implemented sampling-rate recognition and adaptation.
  • Strengthened alignment, missing-data, off-body, and recovery logic while tying accuracy claims to reference evidence.

Measurement science

Neonatal Bilirubin Device Validation

Highlight · Why it matters

Shifted evaluation from correlation alone to measurement agreement, exposing device-, batch-, and range-specific error patterns relevant to calibration decisions.

Key contributions

  • Analyzed paired errors using Bland–Altman reasoning and device-, batch-, and range-level comparisons.
  • Audited calibration designs for split independence, identifiability, reference dependence, and stability.
  • Converted findings into bilingual reports and concrete recommendations for further validation.
