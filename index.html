Research Interests

Reliable learning for real health challenges

I am most energized by problems where statistical rigor and modern AI need each other—where data are imperfect, uncertainty matters, and better evidence can improve healthcare or public health decisions.

01

Longitudinal & event-time modeling

Dynamic prediction from patient histories with unequal length, irregular timing, missing measurements, and changing risk.

02

Wearable & multimodal sensing

Robust representations that account for signal quality, missingness, device heterogeneity, and multiple physiological sources.

03

Uncertainty & calibration

Bayesian reasoning, credible risk estimates, prediction intervals, and evaluation that supports responsible decisions.

04

Healthcare & public health

Methods for heterogeneous populations, rare outcomes, reliable population evidence, and practical digital health research.

How I want to work

Statistics gives me discipline. Machine learning and deep learning give me expressive tools.

I want to combine study design, measurement error analysis, Bayesian ideas, calibration, and uncertainty quantification with flexible temporal models, representation learning, and multimodal AI. My goal is not simply to improve one metric, but to build methods whose claims remain defensible beyond a single dataset.
