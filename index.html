01 · Longitudinal prediction

Delivery-Time Forecasting from Maternal and Fetal Monitoring Data

Modeling repeated, irregular measurements while respecting what was known at each prediction time.

  • Developed temporal models combining physiological signals, gestational age, clinical variables, and patient history.
  • Implemented CNN–Transformer representations and patient-level LSTM components.
  • Designed patient-level evaluation, probabilistic outputs, interval analysis, and subgroup checks.

02 · Heterogeneity & rare outcomes

Preterm Risk and Population-Aware Model Iteration

Testing how different modeling strategies behave across heterogeneous and imbalanced populations.

  • Compared specialist models, soft mixture-of-experts designs, and a unified full-data model.
  • Investigated clinical and learned features for routing and expert-training strategies.
  • Separated patient-level from measurement-level conclusions and documented small-sample limits.

03 · Physiological signals

Fetal Heart Rate Signal Processing and Algorithm Evaluation

Tracing failures from raw signal assumptions through timing, state, and output behavior.

  • Reviewed the processing chain from preprocessing and template matching to heart-rate output.
  • Detected inconsistent sampling assumptions and implemented rate recognition and adaptation.
  • Improved timestamp alignment, missing-value handling, signal-quality gating, and state control.

04 · Measurement science

Neonatal Bilirubin Device Validation and Calibration Analysis

Evaluating whether devices agree—not merely whether their measurements correlate.

  • Analyzed paired measurements across devices, batches, and measurement ranges.
  • Used Bland–Altman reasoning to distinguish average bias from individual agreement.
  • Reviewed calibration designs for independence, identifiability, and reference-device dependence.

05 · Research infrastructure

Biomedical Data Auditing and Human Review Tools

Building a traceable path from database records to analysis-ready signals.

  • Connected measurement IDs, participant records, signal references, and local files.
  • Built resumable download, deduplication, integrity, pairing, and quality-screening workflows.
  • Created a local visualization dashboard for structured human signal review.

Research interests

I want to make health predictions more reliable, transparent, and useful.

I am most energized by problems where statistical rigor and modern AI need each other: longitudinal histories that arrive irregularly, wearable signals with missingness and noise, rare outcomes, population heterogeneity, and predictions whose uncertainty matters. I hope to develop methods that are not only accurate on one dataset, but scientifically defensible and valuable for healthcare and public health.
